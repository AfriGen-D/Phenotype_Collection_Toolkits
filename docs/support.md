# Support

## Free deployment support

Support is available **at no cost** if you're unsure how to deploy the toolkits in REDCap or what you need to use them. Post a request via the [AfriGen-D Helpdesk](https://helpdesk.afrigen-d.org/helpdesk/) under the **Phenotype Standards** queue. One of our team will answer your query or set up a meeting to explore your support needs.

## Report a problem or suggest an improvement

If you find a problem in the design of a toolkit, or have a recommendation, please log an issue on GitHub. Browse the open issues first to see whether someone has already raised it.

## Get involved

We welcome participation from the community! We value ongoing maintenance and improvement of the toolkits and appreciate your feedback, whether it's a bug, a suggestion or a new idea.
